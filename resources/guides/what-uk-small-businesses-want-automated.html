What small businesses usually want automated first

Guide · Getting started

If you're wondering where to start, it helps to know where most small businesses begin. This isn't survey data — it's a practical shortlist of five areas that tend to come up first, because the work is repetitive, rule-based and the payoff is easy to see.

Five common starting points

  1. Chasing late invoices. Reminders that go out on schedule, without awkward emails. See Invoice Chasing Automation.
  2. Getting invoices and receipts into the books. Reading the details and filing them, instead of typing them in. See AI Invoice & Receipt Processing.
  3. Taming the inbox. Sorting mail by type and routing it to the right place. See Email Triage & Routing.
  4. Following up new enquiries. Logging every lead and replying promptly. See Lead Capture → CRM → Follow-Up.
  5. Regular reporting. The same numbers, pulled together on a schedule. See Scheduled Report Generation.

How to choose your first one

Ask three questions about any task you're considering:

  • Does it happen at least weekly?
  • Is it broadly the same steps every time?
  • Does a mistake or a delay cost you money or goodwill?

Start where all three answers are yes. That's usually where the payoff is quickest and the risk is lowest.
