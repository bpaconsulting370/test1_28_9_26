FAQ
What's the difference between Services, Packages and the Shop?
Services are custom-scoped builds. Packages are fixed-scope versions of the most common projects. The Shop will hold ready-made templates you can use without a build.
Do I need technical knowledge to use these automations?
No. We build, test and hand everything over ready to use, with handover documentation — you don't need to touch any code.
What tools do you integrate with?
Most projects connect tools you already use, like Gmail, Outlook, Xero, QuickBooks, HubSpot and Google Sheets. If you use something else, ask — a short discovery call will confirm what's possible.
How long does a typical project take?
Most automations are typically delivered in 5–10 working days from project confirmation, depending on scope.
Is my data safe?
Automations run on your own accounts and permissions, so you stay in control of who has access. Before we start we agree which tools and data are involved; for sensitive documents we can also discuss where AI processing takes place and keep a human check on anything uncertain.
Do you offer support after launch?
Every project includes handover documentation, and ongoing support and new builds are available through the Monthly Automation Retainer.
