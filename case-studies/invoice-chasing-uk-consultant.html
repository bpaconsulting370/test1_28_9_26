Invoice chasing for an independent UK consultant

How automated reminders take late-payment chasing off a solo consultant's plate.

The situation

An independent consultant invoices clients monthly through Xero. Most pay on time, but a handful drift past their due date — and chasing them means writing slightly awkward emails between client calls, when there's rarely a spare moment. Late payments quietly stretch the wait for money that has already been earned.

What we'd set up

Invoice Chasing Automation, connected to Xero, with a reminder sequence the consultant approves before it goes live:

  • A friendly heads-up shortly before the due date
  • A polite reminder when the invoice becomes overdue
  • A firmer follow-up a little later
  • A personal alert to the consultant if it's still unpaid after that — so a human steps in only when it matters

What changes day to day

Reminders go out on schedule without anyone having to remember to send them. The consultant only hears about the invoices that genuinely need a personal conversation, and there's a simple log of what has been sent.
