Document processing for a small accountancy practice

How AI reads incoming receipts and invoices so the team reviews exceptions instead of typing everything.

The situation

A small practice receives client receipts, invoices and statements by email, as photos from phones, and through a shared folder. Someone on the team opens each one, reads the details and types them into the bookkeeping software. It's necessary, repetitive, and error-prone at busy times such as quarter-ends and year-end.

What we'd set up

AI Invoice & Receipt Processing, connected to the practice's inbox, shared folder and bookkeeping software:

  • Documents are picked up automatically from the inbox and the shared folder
  • AI extracts the supplier, date, amount and VAT, and proposes a category
  • High-confidence items are filed directly; anything uncertain is queued for a quick human check
  • Every filed item stays traceable back to the original document

What changes day to day

The team spends its time reviewing exceptions rather than typing every document, and the busy periods stop being quite so busy.

A note on client data

Because these are client financial documents, we'd agree upfront which tools and data are involved and where AI processing takes place, and keep a human check on anything the AI isn't sure about. See our guide to AI automation for UK accountants.
