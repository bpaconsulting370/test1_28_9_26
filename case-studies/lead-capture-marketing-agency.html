Lead capture for a small marketing agency

How every website enquiry gets logged, acknowledged and owned, instead of waiting in an inbox.

The situation

A small agency gets enquiries through its website contact form, LinkedIn messages and referrals. Website enquiries land in a shared inbox, someone copies the details into the CRM when they get a moment, and follow-up depends on who happens to see the message first. Some leads get a reply within minutes; others sit for days.

What we'd set up

Lead Capture → CRM → Follow-Up, connected to the website form and the agency's CRM:

  • Every form submission creates a CRM contact automatically, with the details they entered
  • The lead receives an immediate acknowledgement email the agency has approved
  • The right person is alerted straight away, with the enquiry attached
  • A follow-up reminder is created if no one has replied within a set window

What changes day to day

Every enquiry is logged the moment it arrives, gets a prompt acknowledgement, and has an owner. Nothing depends on someone noticing the inbox at the right moment. As volume grows, AI lead scoring can help the team call the most promising leads first.
