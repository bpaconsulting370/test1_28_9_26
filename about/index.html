About Business Process Automation

We help small businesses reclaim the hours lost to repetitive admin — invoicing, inbox triage, lead follow-up and more — using automation and practical AI. Less time on busywork means more time on the work that actually grows your business.

We work with the tools you already have. Most projects don't need new software, just the software you already pay for, wired together properly.
